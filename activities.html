1. Trekking

Trekking is one of the popular activities in Nepal. People can enjoy beautiful mountains, forests and villages.

2. Visiting Lakes

Nepal has many beautiful lakes. Phewa Lake, Begnas Lake and Rara Lake are some famous lakes.

3. Visiting Historical Places

We can visit temples, palaces and historical monuments to learn about Nepalese history and culture.

4. Wildlife Visit

We can visit national parks and learn about different animals and plants.
